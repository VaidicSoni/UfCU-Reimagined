UFCU Hackathon Challenge: Research and Context

UFCU Core Values (The "UFCU Way")

UFCU's stated purpose is "Empowering our Members to achieve financial success and brighter futures." They emphasize three core values:

Purposefully Member-Obsessed: Empathy-driven, focused on understanding member needs, acting in their best interests, and removing friction.

Possibilities Reimagined: Courageously experimenting, learning, and iterating. Leveraging data and insights to deliver exceptional experiences.

Performance Excellence Rooted in Unwavering Integrity: Championing teamwork, accountability, continuous improvement, and an inclusive environment.

Key Takeaway for Hackathon: Any prototype needs to deeply reflect the "Member-Obsessed" value. It should be personalized, intuitive, and eliminate friction points common in traditional banking.

Target Audience and Demographics

While UFCU's charter is broad, serving individuals and businesses in Travis County (and coastal areas), they have deep roots as a university credit union (University of Texas).

Broad Base: Over 400,000 members across generations (boomer, gen x, millennial, gen z, alpha).

Student Focus: A significant portion of their demographic includes college students (especially from UT). Research on college investors shows they are interested but hesitant, citing a lack of knowledge or fear of losing money.

Key Insight: The onboarding experience needs to cater to a broad range of digital literacy while appealing specifically to a younger, mobile-first demographic that expects speed and transparency.

Current UFCU Mobile Onboarding Process

Based on recent videos of their mobile enrollment process, the current flow includes:

Identification: Requires either a 9-digit UFCU Account Number AND a Date of Birth (DOB) or Social Security Number (SSN/ITIN).

Authentication: Users set up a Username and Password.

Security: Users set up a 5-digit passcode (avoiding consecutive/repeating numbers) and have the option to enable Face ID.

Verification: A "Well Done! Your device is registered successfully" confirmation screen.

Critique of Current Process:

Friction at the Start: Asking for an account number first implies the user has already opened an account elsewhere (e.g., in a branch or online). This isn't a true "net-new" member onboarding from a cold start in the app.

Recent Redesign Feedback: User reviews of the UFCU app (post-2023/2024 redesign) indicate significant frustration with the UI, slow loading times, and confusing navigation (e.g., difficulty seeing balances or making transfers).

Opportunities for the Hackathon

The prompt asks you to "Reimagine UFCU’s New Member Onboarding Experience."

Focus on the "Cold Start": How does someone who is not a member download the app and become a member in minutes?

Identity Verification: The challenge specifically asks how to establish trust without breaking the experience. Look into modern KYC (Know Your Customer) solutions like digital document scanning, liveness checks (selfies), and device fingerprinting.

Product Integration: Don't just open an account; get the user to use it. The prompt mentions applying for products. The prototype should smoothly transition from "account created" to "first deposit made" or "debit card ordered."

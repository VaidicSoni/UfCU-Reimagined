UFCU Expands Access to Financial Services with First Temple Location at Temple College
Published September 9, 2026
|
News Releases
AUSTIN, Texas, September 9, 2026
– UFCU, a leading Texas credit union, announced the opening of its newest full-service branch at
Temple College
, marking the credit union’s first location in the City of Temple and its first on the Temple College campus. The new location expands access to trusted financial services for students, faculty, staff, and the surrounding community.
Located in the Temple College Student Union at 2626 South First Street, the UFCU Temple College branch offers a convenient, welcoming environment where Members can access everyday banking services, lending solutions, and personalized guidance.
“Expanding into Temple allows UFCU to bring our people-focused approach to a new community while building a meaningful relationship with Temple College,” said UFCU President and CEO Michael Crowl. “Our roots are in higher education, and we look forward to empowering more people to achieve financial success and brighter futures.”
The UFCU Temple College branch adds to UFCU’s growing presence across Texas and builds on the credit union’s longstanding commitment to supporting higher education communities. UFCU maintains partnerships with institutions including The University of Texas at Austin, Texas State University, Austin Community College, UTHealth Houston, and the University of Texas Medical Branch in Galveston, creating opportunities to connect students, employees, and their families with financial education and affordable financial solutions.
To learn more about the new Temple College branch location, hours of operation, and services offered, visit
UFCU.org
.
About UFCU
UFCU is a Member-owned, not-for-profit financial institution with more than 433,000 Members and $4 billion in assets. Founded in 1936, UFCU empowers financial success across Texas communities and is dedicated to building a brighter future for all through personalized Member service, innovative products, and financial education.

Rent or Buy?
See How Much U Can Save From Buying
Trying to decide whether to rent or buy your next home? It comes down to more than just a monthly payment. Renting can offer flexibility, while buying helps you build equity over time.
This calculator helps you compare the true saving of buying versus renting based on your financial situation and how long you plan to stay. By looking at both options side by side, you can make a more informed decision about what works best for you.
To get started, enter a few key details:

Annual Renter's Insurance
Buying Cost
Home Purchase Price
Down Payment
Annual Property Tax
Monthly HOA Dues
Annual Homeowners Insurance
Loan Term
Interest Rate
How to use this calculator:
Start by entering your monthly rent and annual renter’s insurance.
Enter the home purchase price and your down payment amount.
Add in ongoing homeownership costs, including property taxes, HOA dues, and annual insurance.
Enter your loan term and interest rate.
Compare the results to see how much you can save by buying instead of renting.

Calculate Home Closing Costs
Estimate Your Home Closing Costs
Buying a home comes with more than just a down payment. Closing costs are the upfront fees and services required to finalize your mortgage and complete your home purchase. Closing costs typically range from 2% to 5% of the home’s purchase price, but your actual costs may vary based on your loan and location.
This calculator helps you estimate your total closing costs so you can plan ahead and avoid surprises at the closing table.
To get started, enter a few key details
Home purchase price
Down payment percent
Origination charges
Discount points
Home appraisal
Credit report
Flood certification
Tax service
Title service
Title insurance
Recording Fees
Transfer Tax
Survey fees
Pest Inspection
How to use this calculator:
Start by entering the home purchase price and your down payment percentage.
Add your lender-related costs, including origination charges and any discount points.
Enter estimated third-party service fees, such as the appraisal, credit report, and flood certification.
Include additional closing services like tax service, title service, and title insurance.
Review your estimated total to better understand how much you may need at closing.
Adjust the values to see how different costs impact your total closing expenses.

UFCU Account Types Master List

UFCU offers a comprehensive suite of banking and loan accounts for personal and business needs.

Personal Checking Accounts:
- Free Checking
- Plus Checking (interest-earning)
- Simply U
- Teen Checking (for ages 13-17)

Personal Savings Accounts:
- Regular Savings
- Money Market Account (tiered rates, bonus dividend options)
- Certificates (often known as CDs, lock in rates for 3-60 months)
- IRAs (Individual Retirement Accounts)
- Youth Accounts: Kidz Savings and Teen Savings

Credit Cards:
- Cash Back Card
- Great Rate Card
- Travel Rewards Card

Personal Loans & Mortgages:
- Home Loans (Mortgages): Purchase loans, First-time homebuyer, Refinance, Home Equity, FHA, VA, Jumbo. 
*Note on Mortgage Accounts: Yes, Mortgage accounts are available! While technically a "loan" rather than a deposit account, members view and pay their mortgage directly through their UFCU digital banking account.*
- Auto Loans
- Personal Loans & Credit Builder Loans
- RV & Boat Loans

Business Checking Accounts:
- Business Checking
- Premium Business Checking

Business Savings Accounts:
- Business Savings
- Premium Business Savings
- Business Money Market
- Business Certificates

Business Loans:
- Auto Loans
- Equipment Loans
- Lines of Credit
- Working Capital

Gap Analysis Note: Health Savings Accounts (HSAs) are not directly offered as standard personal retail accounts, though they are heavily referenced in educational materials and offered as benefits to UFCU staff.

What is Simply U™?
With no monthly fee, no overdraft fees, and fewer requirements to open, Simply U™ is hassle-free banking made easy. And since you can only spend what you deposit, there’s no need to worry about costly overdraft or interest fees.

Is the Simply U™ Account a credit card?
No, Simply U™ is an account that provides simplified banking with all the features you want without the worries of overdraft. You can use Direct Deposit or mobile deposit to fund your account and access funds through your Visa Debit Card. This account does not include check writing.
Opening Your Account
How much does it cost to open a Simply U™ Account?
There is no fee to open a Simply U™ Account.
Can anyone get a Simply U™ Account?
Yes! Simply U™ is for any Member who wants all the basics, without the worry of overspending or monthly fees. A parent or guardian will need to be on the account for anyone under 18 years of age.
How can I get a Simply U™ Account?
Visit any UFCU branch or
sign up online
to open a Simply U™ Account. Once you have opened your account, a personalized card will be issued to you instantly. You may begin using your Simply U™ Account to make purchases immediately. Don’t forget to enroll in Direct Deposit to enjoy the benefit of receiving your paycheck up to two days early.

Can my Simply U™ Account have a negative balance?
A negative balance may occur if the final purchase amount the merchant submits to us is greater than the amount we originally authorized, or the merchant processes a transaction without prior authorization. You should immediately add more money to you Simply U™ Account to bring your balance above zero. There are no overdraft fees for the Simply U™ Account.
Can I deposit funds into my Simply U™ Account through UFCU Mobile Deposit?
Yes, with a capable mobile device. Check deposits using UFCU Mobile Deposit are subject to the Funds Availability Policy.

Can I use my Simply U™ card internationally? Is there a cost or fee for doing so?
Yes, for your convenience you have access to your accounts internationally via our
National & International Shared Branches
.
You can also use your Simply U™ Card internationally wherever VISA® Debit cards are accepted. If the purchase is not completed in U.S. Dollars, it will be converted or exchanged to U.S. Dollars. The exchange rate will be applied either by the network that processes your transaction or the government-mandated rate in effect for the date that the network processes your transaction, which may be on a date that is different than when you used the card. In addition, a VISA® 1% International Service Fee will be added to the converted amount.
Can I get cash at the register when I am making a purchase with my Simply U™ Debit card?
You can get cash back at a checkout terminal during a PIN-based transaction if the merchant supports it. Just select "Debit" or "ATM" at the register.
Can I split a purchase, paying with my Simply U™ Account card and a portion in cash?
You may do so if the merchant allows it.
Can my Simply U™ Account purchases be declined?
Most merchants ask us to authorize your purchase. When we give authorization to a merchant, we will hold funds on your Simply U™ Account, generally for up to 2 business days, to pay for your purchase. For gas station purchases, up to $100 may be temporarily reserved when you use your debit card to pay at the pump dependent on the merchant. To avoid a declined purchase or incurring a hold greater than your actual purchase, pay for gas purchases at the cashier.
When swiping my card, should I select "Credit" or "Debit"?
You have a choice, and it is always up to you:
If you select "Credit," you will not need to provide your PIN and you may need to sign a receipt.
If you select "Debit" or "ATM," you will need to enter your PIN. You can use the "Debit" option to withdraw cash with your purchase.

Visit ufcu.org, and
download a Direct Deposit form

UFCU's Routing & Transit (R&T) number is 314977405.
Are there any limits on transactions?
Yes. You may withdraw up to $1,000 cash per day from the ATM, and you may make purchases totaling up to $2,000 per day.

Is there a monthly fee for a Simply U™ Account?
No, the Simply U™ account does not carry a monthly service fee.
Does the Simply U™ Account Debit Card expire?
Yes. A new Simply U™ card will be mailed to your address on file before your current Simply U™ card expires. You will need to follow the activation instructions included with that card before you can use your new card. To ensure your new card reaches you, always notify UFCU immediately of an address change.

Yes. Please
contact us

A Financial Boost When You Need It
UFCU is a trustworthy partner through thick and thin. With excellent rates and no hidden fees, our Personal Loans
1
are helpful for one-time expenses, holiday spending, taxes, or other bills.
2
One option is a Personal Line of Credit
1
that gives you access to emergency cash or extra overdraft protection. Transfer funds into your checking account to cover payments that exceed your available balance. Pay interest only on the amount you’ve used; your Line of Credit is automatically replenished as you make payments.
We also offer signature (unsecured) and share- or certificate-secured Personal Loans. With a secured loan, your current account serves as security, guaranteeing your loan and unlocking lower interest rates. Your account continues to earn dividends. We charge rates that are based on the dividend rate of the securing account. As you pay down the loan, the securing funds become available for your use.
A Personal Loan is an excellent alternative to payday loans. Don’t let a temporary bump in the road derail your path to financial success.

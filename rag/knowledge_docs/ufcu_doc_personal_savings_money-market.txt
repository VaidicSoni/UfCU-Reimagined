Ideal for Higher Yield, Short-Term Savings
A Money Market Account lets you maximize your earnings at a higher rate than a regular savings account while accessing your funds anytime without penalty. Keep access to your funds when you need them through Online Banking and the Mobile app.
Your funds are safe with NCUA federal deposit insurance, which covers up to $250,000 per individual depositor.

Qualifying accounts can earn a higher dividend with our Bonus Dividend option: Earn more on balances less than $10,000 in Money Market Share deposits. We’ll automatically apply your bonus dividend rate when you perform at least 10 qualifying checking account transactions in a single month, including debit card purchases.

FAQs
What is a money market account and how does it work?
A money market account offers flexible, high-yield savings that earns interest while continuing to provide access to your funds any time, day or night. You can access your funds without penalty anytime.
How can I access my money market funds?
You can view and access your available money market funds immediately through UFCU Online Banking, Mobile Banking, Phone Banking, or by stopping by any location.
Is there a minimum balance required for a money market account?
There is a minimum deposit required to open a money market account ($2,500). When money market accounts have a zero balance, they will be closed automatically. But any account with a balance of $.01 or more can remain open.
How is interest paid?
Interest is posted monthly to your money market account. The transaction description will appear in your account history and statement as “dividend.”

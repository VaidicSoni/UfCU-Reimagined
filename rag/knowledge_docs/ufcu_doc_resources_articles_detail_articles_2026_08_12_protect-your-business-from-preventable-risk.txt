Protect Your Business from Preventable Risk
Get simple, practical steps to help protect your business from fraud, payment scams, and unauthorized activity.
Updated August 19, 2026
Published August 12, 2026
Prioritize Your Security
Fraud can move quickly, but clear processes and strong habits can help you stay ahead of it. Follow these best practices for business fraud prevention and payment fraud prevention.
Monitor Activity Regularly
The sooner you spot unusual activity, the more you can limit its impact.
Review your accounts frequently
Look for unexpected transactions or changes.
Set up alerts to get notified of unusual activity so you can act quickly.
Strengthen Payment Security
Payment workflows are a common target for fraud.
Use secure, trusted payment methods
Avoid storing sensitive card data unnecessarily
Confirm any payment changes independently
Pause before processing payments that feel unusual.
Use Strong Authentication
Protect access to your systems and financial tools.
Require unique logins for employees>
Enable multi-factor authentication across platforms
Limit access to only what’s necessary
Train Your Team
Your people are one of your strongest defenses.
Teach employees to recognize phishing and impersonation attempts
Establish a clear process for verifying requests
Encourage a “pause and confirm” mindset
Secure Devices and Systems
Keep your infrastructure protected.
Keep systems and software updated
Use secure networks when accessing financial data
Monitor vendor access and system integrations
Protect Your Physical and Digital Assets
Fraud isn’t always digital.
Store cards, checks, and documents securely
Monitor access to payment systems
Act quickly if something is lost or missing
Act Quickly If Something Feels Off
Speed matters, but so does clarity. If you notice unusual activity:
Stop using affected systems or payment methods
Review accounts and credentials
Contact UFCU immediately
Stay in Control
Fraud prevention isn’t about reacting. It’s about building habits that reduce risk over time.
Pause before acting.
Verify before processing.
Protect what you’ve built.
Visit the Security Center
for step-by-step guidance when something doesn’t feel right.

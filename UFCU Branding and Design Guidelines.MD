Typography and Style

While a specific font isn't mandated in the prompt, credit unions typically use clean, modern, and accessible sans-serif fonts (like Inter, Roboto, or Open Sans).

Voice/Tone: Friendly, empowering, transparent, and simple ("The UFCU Way").

UI Design Solutions for the Prototype

Based on the research and competitor analysis, here are specific UI/UX solutions you can incorporate into your Figma design and web app:

The "Welcome" Screen (Pre-Onboarding):

Don't start with "Enter SSN."

Start with a warm welcome and a value proposition: "Join UFCU in under 3 minutes."

Ask a single, engaging question: "What brings you to UFCU today?" (Options: Everyday Banking, Buying a Home, Auto Loan, etc.). This personalizes the rest of the flow.

The Progress Indicator:

Include a persistent, simple progress bar at the top of the screen (e.g., "Step 1 of 3: About You").

Identity Verification (The "Trust" Element):

Design a mock ID scanning interface.

Include text reassuring the user: "Bank-level encryption keeps your data safe."

Add a simulated "Liveness Check" screen (e.g., "Position your face in the oval").

Data Input Optimization:

Use auto-formatting for phone numbers and SSNs.

Use address auto-complete APIs (mocked in the prototype) to save typing.

The "Activation" State:

After the account is approved, the next screen should not be a dead end.

Provide immediate options: "Fund your account," "Order your debit card," or "Set up direct deposit."

Use Plaid (or mock a Plaid interface) to show how easily they can transfer an initial deposit from an external bank.

Addressing Past UI Complaints:

Recent user reviews complained about cluttered interfaces and difficulty finding balances.

Ensure your final "Dashboard" screen (where the user lands after onboarding) is minimalist. The Primary Navy Blue should be used for headers, with plenty of white space, and the Secondary Orange used sparingly for primary Call to Action (CTA) buttons.

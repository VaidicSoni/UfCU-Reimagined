Frequently Asked Questions
Is there a minimum balance required?
No minimum balance is required to keep your account open or avoid fees. No hidden balance requirements apply at any time.
How does early direct deposit work?
Eligible employers and payment providers send funds early once enrolled in direct deposit. Timing depends on employer participation.
Can the debit card be replaced quickly?
Instant-issue debit cards are available in branches, including replacements if needed. Replacement cards can be issued in person for speed.
What tools help manage the account daily?
Mobile alerts, card controls, and online tools help track spending and account activity. Notifications help keep spending visible.

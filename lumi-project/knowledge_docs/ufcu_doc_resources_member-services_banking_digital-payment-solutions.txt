Digital Payment Services
Add Your Card to Your Digital Wallet
With the UFCU Mobile app, add your UFCU Debit or Credit card to Apple
®
or Google™ wallet with the tap of a button and enjoy more ways to pay.
Convenience for U
Digital wallets let you pay with just a tap — no more insert or swipe errors. Better yet, when you tap-to-pay, it only takes seconds, allowing you to get your next coffee with ease.
Keeping U Secure
Digital Wallet is secure and uses biometric technology and tokenization to protect your account. Your card number is never shared with merchants.
Pay In-Store, Online, or In-app
To pay in-store, look for the Contactless Symbol at the checkout and hold your phone near the contactless reader. A checkmark with the word ‘done’ will be displayed once the payment is complete.
To pay online or in-app, select your preferred digital wallet and verify your information.

AD&D Insurance
Accidental Death and Dismemberment (AD&D) insurance, underwritten by Minnesota Life Insurance Company, is a convenient way to protect yourself and your family. You may even be eligible to receive $3,000 of coverage at no cost.
UFCU and Minnesota Life offer up to $300,000 of AD&D insurance at group rates. Get guaranteed acceptance for up to $300,000 additional coverage. UFCU Members 18 and older receive the following benefits:
Preauthorization for up to $300,000 in additional coverage
No doctor visits, health questions, or lab tests required
No disqualification due to age or health
Activate this important insurance now (underwritten by Minnesota Life Insurance Company — all coverage reduced by 50% at age 70, regardless of age at enrollment). Make sure your family has help financially when you’re no longer with them.

Mexico Insurance
Protect your vacation mindset — and your financial well-being — with insurance for your vehicle while traveling in Mexico. UFCU can help you find the right policy for your travel plans.
Coverage is available for physical damage, total theft and vandalism of your vehicle, as well as your personal liability, legal assistance, medical payments, roadside assistance, medical evacuation and return tickets home.
